ESC Range Calibration
⚠ Disconnect the weapon motor before calibrating!
Full-range calibration teaches the ESC the minimum and maximum signal endpoints. Most ESCs will beep to confirm each step.
Full Range (High → Low)
  1. Disconnect the weapon motor from the ESC
  2. Press Start — max throttle (2000 µs) is output for 3 s
  3. Controller automatically drops to min throttle (1000 µs) for 3 s
  4. Reconnect the motor once calibration completes
