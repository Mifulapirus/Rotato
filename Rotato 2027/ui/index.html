ESC range calibration

⚠ Disconnect the weapon motor before calibrating.
Teaches the ESC the minimum and maximum throttle signal. Most ESCs beep to confirm each step.
  1. Disconnect the weapon motor from the ESC
  2. Press Start: full throttle (2000 µs) for 3 s
  3. The robot drops to minimum (1000 µs) for 3 s
  4. Reconnect the motor when it says complete
